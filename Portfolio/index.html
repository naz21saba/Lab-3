01 — about

The side projects became
the main story.

I studied computer science, but I never stopped making things on the side — and eventually the side projects became the main story.

My days split between two very different kinds of problem-solving. Some mornings I’m deep in a NestJS backend or debugging an auth flow. Other mornings I’m researching Etsy search trends, refining a product workflow or writing a Pinterest-ready article.

I’ve found the two feed each other more than people expect. The same instinct for structure and detail shows up whether I’m naming a database column, mapping an automation or naming a product listing.

I’m based in Pakistan, self-taught in many of the tools I use daily, and I genuinely enjoy the operational side of running small creative businesses as much as the craft itself.

How I thinkStart with the system, remove friction, then make the experience feel simple.
What I likeBackend logic, useful internal tools, product workflows, SEO, editorial design and scalable content systems.
What connects itTurning repetitive or messy processes into something structured, usable and easier to grow.
