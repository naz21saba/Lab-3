Keywords— component, formatting, style, conference paper, research

I. Introduction

This section introduces the research problem and explains why the topic is important. Replace this text with your own introduction. IEEE conference papers normally use numbered main headings with uppercase Roman numerals.

The paper should clearly explain the motivation, background, objective, and contribution of the work. Keep the formatting consistent throughout the document.

II. Ease of Use

A. Selecting a Template

Confirm that the correct paper size and conference template are being used. This HTML example is visually based on the US Letter conference template.

B. Maintaining the Integrity of the Specifications

Do not change the margins, column widths, fonts, or spacing without a clear reason. These specifications are intended to keep all papers visually consistent.

III. Prepare Your Paper Before Styling

Complete the paper content before final formatting. Proofread the writing, organize all sections, and prepare graphics separately before inserting them into the final document.

A. Abbreviations and Acronyms

Define abbreviations the first time they are used in the paper unless they are already widely understood.

B. Units

  • Use SI units whenever appropriate.
  • Avoid mixing incompatible unit systems.
  • Write a zero before decimal points, for example 0.25.

C. Equations

Number equations consecutively and position the equation number flush right.

a + b = γ (1)

D. Some Common Mistakes

Proofread terminology, punctuation, symbols, abbreviations, and technical language before submission.

IV. Using the Template

A. Authors and Affiliations

Author names should be listed from left to right and then continue on the next row. Keep affiliations short and consistent.

B. Identify the Headings

Headings guide the reader through the paper. Main text headings use Roman numerals while subordinate headings use letters.

C. Figures and Tables

Place figures and tables near the top or bottom of columns whenever possible. Figure captions belong below figures, while table titles appear above tables.
